# Privacy Policy **Last updated: September 30, 2026** Timiza ("Timiza", "we", "us", or "our") provides software for schools and educational organizations to manage students, teachers, classes, lessons, schedules, attendance, assignments, resources, and related educational activities. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the Timiza service. ## 1. Information We Collect Depending on how you use Timiza, we may collect: * Name and contact information. * Email address and account credentials. * User role, such as administrator, teacher, or student. * School and class information. * Lesson, attendance, assignment, assessment, and scheduling information. * Information submitted through the application. * Technical information required to operate and secure the service. We only collect information that is reasonably necessary to provide and operate the service. ## 2. Google Account and Google Calendar Integration Timiza may allow teachers or other authorized users to connect their Google account to enable calendar and online-lesson functionality. When you choose to connect a Google account, Timiza may request access to Google Calendar information permitted by the OAuth authorization screen. Depending on the functionality you authorize, Timiza may use this access to: * Create calendar events for scheduled lessons. * Update or manage calendar events created through Timiza. * Associate online lesson information with calendar events. * Create or associate Google Meet conferencing information when supported by the integration. Timiza only requests Google permissions that are necessary for the functionality being provided. You can revoke Timiza's access to your Google account through your Google account settings or by disconnecting the integration where that functionality is available in Timiza. ## 3. How We Use Information We may use collected information to: * Provide and operate Timiza. * Create and manage user accounts. * Manage schools, classes, teachers, and students. * Schedule and manage lessons. * Provide attendance, assignment, assessment, and educational management features. * Provide online-lesson and calendar functionality. * Communicate with users about the service. * Provide customer support. * Detect, investigate, and prevent unauthorized access, fraud, abuse, or security incidents. * Maintain and improve the service. * Comply with applicable legal obligations. ## 4. Google API Data When you connect a Google account, Timiza may receive information from Google APIs according to the permissions you authorize. Timiza's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with applicable Google API Services User Data Policy requirements. Timiza does not sell Google user data. Google data obtained through an authorized integration is used only to provide or improve the functionality for which the user granted permission. ## 5. Sharing of Information We do not sell personal information. We may use trusted service providers to operate Timiza, including providers for hosting, databases, authentication, email, storage, monitoring, security, and other infrastructure. Such providers may process information only as necessary to provide services to Timiza. We may also disclose information where required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect the rights, security, and property of Timiza, its users, or others. ## 6.
